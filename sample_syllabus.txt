Course: Introduction to Computer Science

UNIT I: Computer Fundamentals
Characteristics and applications of computers; generations of computers;
basic computer organization; input, output, and storage devices.

UNIT II: Number Systems and Data Representation
Binary, decimal, octal, and hexadecimal number systems; conversions between
number systems; binary arithmetic; character representation.

UNIT III: Introduction to Programming
Algorithms and flowcharts; variables and data types; operators; conditional
statements; loops; functions; basic debugging.
